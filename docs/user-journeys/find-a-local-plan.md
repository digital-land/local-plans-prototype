# Find a local plan

**Who:** anyone, no sign in needed.

**Goal:** find a planning authority's local plans and see what we know about them.

## Steps

1. Go to the home page.
2. Choose "View or enter housing number data for planning authorities".
3. Find the planning authority in the list. Each authority shows its plans with their stage
   (for example emerging or adopted).
4. Choose the plan.

## What they should see

- The planning authority's page, with a tab for each of its plans.
- For the chosen plan: its title, planning policy URL, plan period and local development
  framework number.
- No editing controls. Instead there is a "Login to edit this page" link.
